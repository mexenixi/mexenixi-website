Product: Sort & Done!
Shared product identifier: Narabi
Provider: Mexenixi
Overview: An iPhone and iPad app for importing photos, scans, PDFs, and supported documents as pages, arranging them freely, and exporting them as PDF or images.
Availability approach: Every feature is free. No ads, account, analytics SDK, or tracking SDK. Optional support purchases do not unlock features or content.
Main features: Photo, file, scan, and PDF import; independent pageization for supported documents; workspace and tray; multi-selection; drag and drop; undo and redo; rotation, crop, color adjustment, document correction, paper placement, layer composition; PDF, JPEG, PNG, transparent PNG, and photo-library export; AI handoff PDF and text creation; on-device project storage.
Documents: DOCX, XLSX, and PPTX input is not a full Microsoft Office compatibility conversion. The app creates independent pages suited to review and rearrangement. Documents that cannot be parsed safely are not added. RTF and RTFD are unsupported.
Privacy: Projects and OCR results are not automatically sent to a developer server. AI handoff files are not uploaded automatically and the user chooses the destination. The destination service's terms apply after sharing.
Limitations: Complete accuracy of OCR, document pageization, external AI answers, and exports is not guaranteed. Undo history is not guaranteed after relaunch. Cloud synchronization is not a core feature.
