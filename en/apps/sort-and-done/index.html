Sort & Done!

Put photos and PDFs in the order you want. Import, arrange, refine, and export with every feature available for free.

All features freeNo adsNo account21 languages

What it does

Import

Bring in photos, images, scans, PDFs, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, and supported text or structured-data files as pages.

Arrange

Organize pages with the workspace and tray, drag and drop, multi-selection, and undo or redo.

Export

Export in the selected order as PDF, JPEG, PNG, transparent PNG, or save images to the photo library.

Independent document pageization

DOCX, XLSX, and PPTX files are not converted with full Microsoft Office layout compatibility. Sort & Done! reads useful content such as text, tables, sheets, and slides and creates its own pages for review and rearrangement. Export the source document as PDF first when visual fidelity is essential.

Privacy

Projects and OCR results are not automatically sent to a developer server. AI handoff files are never uploaded automatically, and you choose where to share them.
