Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

Mexenixi, operated by Mizuki Kubo, describes the handling of information in Sort & Done! below.

1. Core approach

The app does not use accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs. Photos, documents, projects, and OCR results are designed to be processed locally on the device.

2. Information stored on the device

The app stores projects, pages, editing state, export settings, app preferences, and state needed to display purchase acknowledgements. Deleting the app may also delete app data stored on the device.

3. Photos, camera, and files

The app processes only items you select or capture. It does not intentionally overwrite source files. Saving to the photo library occurs only when you request it.

4. OCR and document pageization

OCR uses Apple Vision on the device. Supported document pageization also runs on the device and does not guarantee exact formatting compatibility or content accuracy.

5. AI handoff files and sharing

AI handoff PDFs and text files are created only at your request and are never uploaded automatically. You choose the destination in the system share sheet. The destination service's terms and privacy policy apply after sharing.

6. Optional support purchases

Purchases are processed through Apple's StoreKit. The developer does not receive payment-card details.

7. Support messages

A support email contains information and attachments you choose to send. Review personal or confidential information before sending.
